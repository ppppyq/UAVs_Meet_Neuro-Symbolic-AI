## 1. Neural part

A vision-language model processes RGB-D observations and constructs a local
semantic map of objects and attributes. An LLM selects optimal binary queries
for the human operator.

## 2. Symbolic part

MINT is an explicit neuro-symbolic tree representing obstacle and goal
knowledge gaps. A hierarchical planner evaluates trajectory cost, trajectory
divergence, and goal entropy over hypothesis branches.

## 3. Coupling

The VLM identifies semantic uncertainty; MINT structures that uncertainty as a
symbolic tree; the LLM chooses a query; the human's binary response prunes the
tree and updates the semantic map and plan. The loop is bidirectional.

## 4. UAV validation

The full text reports NVIDIA Isaac simulation and real-world quadrotor
deployment with a voice-interactive workflow. The real-world study is
feasibility-oriented rather than a broad controlled benchmark.

## 5. Limitations

No official code repository was identified. Real-world results are not broad
controlled benchmarks, and the paper does not provide evidence for multi-UAV
distributed reasoning.
