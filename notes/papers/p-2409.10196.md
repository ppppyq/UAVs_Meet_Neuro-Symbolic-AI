## 1. Neural part

GRiD is adapted from HYDRA and integrates vision foundation models, a
fine-tuned CLIP classifier for object attributes and types, 2D tracking, and
LLM-generated reasoner code. It consumes RGB, depth, and GPS data and grounds
entities of interest in 3D.

## 2. Symbolic part

The probabilistic world model maintains explicit environment state: it applies
physical constraints, Bayesian position filtering, discrete attribute ranking,
and reporting over tracked objects. SNaC is a hierarchical symbolic planner
with high-level area selection, mid-level navigation, and low-level coverage.

## 3. Coupling

GRiD's neural outputs are noisy symbolic detections and attributes. The world
model reasons over and accumulates those symbols into a belief map, which SNaC
then consumes for planning. The reviewed direction is neural-to-symbolic, with
symbolic world state feeding the symbolic planner.

## 4. UAV validation

The paper evaluates NEUSIS on HAMERITT urban search missions in a 500m x 500m
AirSim/Unreal environment. The reviewed evidence is simulation only; no
real-flight validation was identified.

## 5. Limitations

No real-flight results were found. The official repository
(https://github.com/ControlNet/NEUSIS) was located, but replication status and
license were not fully audited. The record remains `candidate`, not `included`.
