# NEUSIS: A Compositional Neuro-Symbolic Framework for Autonomous Perception, Reasoning, and Planning in Complex UAV Search Missions

## Neural–symbolic interface

GRiD integrates vision foundation models, a fine-tuned CLIP classifier for object attributes/types, 2D tracking, and LLM-generated reasoner code adapted from HYDRA to ground objects in 3D from RGB, depth, and GPS.

A probabilistic world model accumulates noisy GRiD detections with Bayesian position filtering, discrete attribute ranking, physical constraints, and explicit reporting; SNaC performs hierarchical selection, navigation, and coverage planning.

GRiD neural perception produces noisy symbolic detections and attributes; the probabilistic world model reasons over and accumulates those symbols, then SNaC consumes the updated belief map for hierarchical planning.

## Evidence and limitations

GRiD neural perception and generated programs feed a persistent probabilistic world model; MiniZinc AOI selection and hierarchical navigation guide search. AirSim/HAMERITT comparisons and component ablations are reported.

Simulation only. The world model starts with ground-truth occupancy/BEV maps and relies on accurate localization/depth. Table I SR measures entity localization within 5 m, not whole-mission success or flight safety; false positives require separate F1/precision analysis.

## Classification

Primary theme is perception and world modeling because GRiD grounding and the probabilistic world model are the central coupling; SNaC planning and navigation are secondary.

Meets the four scope criteria after source-based review of the neural component, explicit symbolic mechanism, functional coupling and described UAV validation. Inclusion is a review decision, not human review or independent replication.
