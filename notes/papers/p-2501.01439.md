# Probabilistic Mission Design for Neuro-Symbolic Unmanned Aircraft Systems

Reviewed 2026-09-22 · fulltext_reviewed · included · human review pending.

## Neural–symbolic interface

ChangeFormer estimates change probabilities; an LLM translates operator requirements.

Hybrid probabilistic logic programs over explicit spatial relations and mission constraints.

Neural probabilities become logic facts; inference produces a spatial field of modeled requirement satisfaction.

## Evidence and limitations

ProMis connects uncertain maps and neural sensing to declarative mission rules. Evaluation uses geographic case studies, satellite-image change detection and runtime/sampling experiments, not closed-loop flight trials.

Pointwise model satisfaction is not a certified flight-path probability or legal authorization. Results depend on uncertainty inputs, encoded rules and inference cost; no HIL or real-flight experiment was identified.
