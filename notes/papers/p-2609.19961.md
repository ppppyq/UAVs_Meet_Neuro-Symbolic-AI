## 1. Neural part

The article describes neural modules that interpret multimodal observations,
mission instructions, and contextual inputs, and that ground objects, states,
and reusable skills. It does not identify a single named neural model with
training details or standalone benchmark metrics in the reviewed HTML
full-text.

## 2. Symbolic part

The reference architecture includes explicit task/goal management, symbolic
rules and skill preconditions, neuro-symbolic planning,
verification/metacognition, shared knowledge/memory, and explicit evidence
conditions. These are presented as architectural components, not as a
standalone symbolic theorem set or logic implementation.

## 3. Coupling

Neural grounding feeds an agentic symbolic planning and verification loop.
Execution and connectivity feedback updates task state and can trigger sensing
or replanning. The article presents the closed loop as an architecture rather
than as a measured algorithm-level integration study.

## 4. UAV validation

The reviewed full text uses an illustrative LAESim urban fire-inspection case
to demonstrate evidence-grounded skill reuse. The record therefore carries
`conceptual` and `simulation` evidence. No independent quantitative benchmark
table, hardware-in-the-loop study, or real-flight validation was identified.

## 5. Limitations

This is a position/architecture article rather than a benchmarked method. The
use case is qualitative, and there is no controlled comparison of accuracy,
safety, latency, or resource use. It is kept as `candidate` and must not be
counted as a core included UAV method.
