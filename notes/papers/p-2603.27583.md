## 1. Neural part

From the abstract, a reasoning-enhanced large language model translates
free-form natural language into executable Signal Temporal Logic
specifications using chain-of-thought supervision and group-relative policy
optimization.

## 2. Symbolic part

Signal Temporal Logic specifications and mixed-integer linear programming are
used for trajectory synthesis. A repair mechanism combines MILP-based
diagnosis with LLM-guided semantic reasoning and claims to enforce safety
guarantees.

## 3. Coupling

The abstract describes LLM-to-STL translation followed by MILP trajectory
synthesis, with infeasibility diagnosis fed back into LLM-guided repair. This
is neural-to-symbolic at translation and symbolic-to-neural at repair.

## 4. UAV validation

The abstract reports extensive simulations and real-world flight experiments.
These claims are author-reported at abstract level only.
