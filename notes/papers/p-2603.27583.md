## Neural–symbolic interface

Qwen2.5-0.5B translator adapted using LoRA/SFT and GRPO; an LLM selects repair dimensions.

STL formulas, mixed-integer trajectory constraints, IIS conflict diagnosis and protected safety predicates.

The LLM proposes STL; solver conflicts guide temporal/predicate repair while the optimization layer chooses relaxation magnitudes.

## Evidence and limitations

Links language translation, constrained trajectory synthesis and specification repair. Table I reports 98% normalized formula exact match; a separate outdoor DJI Matrice 300 RTK demonstration is described.

Known geometry and simplified dynamics; translation/planning run offboard. Formula exact match is not semantic equivalence or a flight-safety rate. The outdoor example does not establish broad operational robustness; formal encoding and repair assumptions require independent audit.

Meets the four scope criteria after source-based review of the neural component, explicit symbolic mechanism, functional coupling and described UAV validation. Inclusion is a review decision, not human review or independent replication.
