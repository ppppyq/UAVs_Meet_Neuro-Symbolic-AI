\section{Foundations and Conceptual Boundaries}

Neural learning is treated as learned function approximation or perception;
symbolic structure is an explicit representation with logic, rules, programs,
or semantically meaningful constraints. Coupling means the two components
affect inference or learning together. General neural-symbolic foundations
\cite{p_2012_05876,p_1805_10872} inform these boundaries. Using an LLM, agent,
knowledge graph, controller, or formal verifier is not automatically
neuro-symbolic AI.
