\section{Introduction and Research Questions}

Trustworthy UAV autonomy needs both learned perception and explicit, auditable
reasoning. This working review asks: how can neural learning and explicit
symbolic representations, reasoning, and constraints be integrated to support
trustworthy UAV autonomy?

Adjacent surveys describe LLM/foundation-model and agentic UAV systems
\cite{p_2501_02341,p_2506_08045}, while earlier neuro-symbolic foundations
provide general integration mechanisms \cite{p_2012_05876,p_1805_10872}.
The present work is still a manuscript in preparation; it does not claim
novelty, exhaustiveness, or experimental results.
