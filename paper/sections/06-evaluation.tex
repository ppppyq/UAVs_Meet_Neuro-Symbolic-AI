\section{Evaluation, Benchmarks, and Evidence Quality}

Simulation, hardware-in-the-loop, and real-flight evidence must remain
separate. The landing-assessment candidate reports 61 successful assessments
out of 72 simulated scenarios and 100 hardware-in-the-loop profiling trials,
but no real-flight validation was identified in the reviewed full text
\cite{p_2607_02277}. The architecture article describes an illustrative
simulation use case rather than a standalone benchmark \cite{p_2609_19961}.
This project does not construct a numerical leaderboard without controlled
comparison data.
