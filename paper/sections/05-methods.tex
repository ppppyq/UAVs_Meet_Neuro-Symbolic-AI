\section{Methods Across the UAV Autonomy Stack}

The current direct UAV records are organized into five reader-facing research
themes rather than a single functional-position hierarchy: perception and
world modeling, reasoning and mission planning, navigation and control, safety
and verification, and collaboration and interaction. Primary theme assignment
is mutually exclusive; secondary theme links are cross-references only.

Perception and world modeling is represented by NEUSIS, which couples GRiD
visual grounding with a probabilistic world model and hierarchical planning
\cite{p_2409_10196}. Reasoning and mission planning is represented by a
position/architecture article for networked low-altitude UAVs
\cite{p_2609_19961}; it is not counted as a standalone core method. Navigation
and control is represented by an LLM-to-STL translation and repair method
\cite{p_2603_27583}, currently reviewed at abstract level. Safety and
verification is represented by a landing-site assessment method combining
quantized segmentation, a probabilistic semantic scene graph, and
deterministic symbolic safety rules \cite{p_2607_02277}. Collaboration and
interaction is represented by MINT, which uses a neuro-symbolic tree and LLM
queries for human--UAV knowledge elicitation \cite{p_2603_07824}.

No direct UAV method has yet been promoted to an included core method.
