\section{Methods Across the UAV Autonomy Stack}

The current direct UAV records illustrate different maturity levels. One is a
position/architecture article for networked low-altitude UAVs with an
illustrative use case \cite{p_2609_19961}. Another is a landing-site assessment
method combining quantized segmentation, a probabilistic semantic scene graph,
and deterministic symbolic safety rules \cite{p_2607_02277}; its venue metadata
is still unresolved. Neither record has yet been promoted to an included core
method.
