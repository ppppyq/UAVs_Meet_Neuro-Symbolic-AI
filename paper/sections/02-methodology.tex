\section{Review Methodology and Scope}

The review separates metadata verification, abstract review, full-text review,
version provenance, and evidence location. The source of truth is
\texttt{data/papers.json}; generated tables, the static site, and BibTeX
entries are produced by the maintenance script. Inclusion requires documented
neural learning, an explicit symbolic component, functional coupling, and UAV
validation scope. Current candidates remain screened rather than automatically
included when full-text evidence or metadata is incomplete.
